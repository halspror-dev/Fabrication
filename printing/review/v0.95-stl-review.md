# v0.95 STL review for the P2S (roadmap step 6)

Reviewed 2026-10-29. The 8 STLs were rebuilt locally from `main` (`cd drawing-scripts && python3 make_stl.py
templates.py /tmp/stl`) and measured with `printing/tools/stl_review.py` (trimesh, layer slices at 0.20 mm). Nothing
was uploaded anywhere. `templates.py` 57 PASS, 0 FAIL; `check3d.py` PASS in all 5 states (min gap 0.03").

Labels: **[M]** measured on the mesh by the tool (geometry, not a print), **[S]** sourced (URL or a step 3 research
file), **[F]** Fabrication's target table (project-notes `archive/printing/2026-10-29-fabrication-fit-tolerances.md`),
**[I]** our inference. Nothing is printed yet, so nothing here is measured on a part.

## Summary

All 8 meshes are clean: watertight, consistent winding, one body each, no degenerate faces **[M]**. Every part is
already in its best orientation: plates flat with every fit hole vertical, sleeves standing on their open side with
the hand surface as the top dome. No part needs supports **[M]** (only overhangs: the sleeves' 5.1 mm screw-hole roofs
and 3.4 mm slot ceilings, far under the TDS 30 mm bridge limit **[S]** `design-strength.md`). Layer rounding at
0.20 mm lands every thickness in Fabrication's band: collar 2.20 (11 layers), plates 3.20 (16), shims 0.80 (4) **[M]**.

The risks are fits, one thin wall, and slicer behaviour that our calibration coupon would not have shown. Ranked by
risk to the print test:

## 1. Tab slots vs. printed plate thickness (highest risk)

- **Evidence:** collar tab slots are closed 8.64 x 3.251 mm holes **[M]**; the tabs are the frame's thickness, which
  prints as layers: 3.20 mm at 0.20 mm **[M]**. That leaves 0.05 mm of play. `templates.py` itself reports "snug, file
  to fit; play 0.003" (0.08 mm against the metal 3.175). Holes and slots print 0.1-0.35 mm small uncompensated
  **[S]** `design-strength.md`. So an uncompensated collar will not take the tabs. Fabrication's band: 3.25-3.35,
  never under the printed plate thickness **[F]**.
- **Why it is worse than it looks:** this is the one fit that pairs an XY size (slot, set by hole compensation) with a
  Z size (tab thickness, set by layers and first-layer squish). Our coupon C4 prints the slot, and C5 prints the plate
  thickness, but separately **[I]**.
- **Fix (printing):** in batch 2 T9, push a printed C5 plate square into the C4 3.25 slot, not only the metal plate.
  Set the collar's hole compensation so the slot lands at 3.30 **[F]** midpoint, then check the 6.35 pin hole still
  lands 6.35-6.45. If one value can't do both, favour the pin hole and file the four slots to fit (Fabrication's
  `SLOT_W` note already says "file to fit").

## 2. Pivot lobe neck vs. hole compensation (frame)

- **Evidence:** the four pivot lobes are one closed hole, 6.35 x 19.05 mm, with necks of 4.77 / 4.73 / 4.71 mm **[M]**
  (Fabrication rounds them to 4.83 = 0.19 in; limit "under 5.3" **[F]**). Growing the hole by XY hole compensation
  (per side) gives necks of: +0.10 → 4.97-5.03, +0.15 → 5.10-5.16, +0.20 → 5.23-5.29 **[M]**. The neck grows about
  2.6 mm per mm of compensation, against 2 mm for a round hole, because the cusps recede along the lobes **[M]**.
- **Nozzle rounding:** the cusps are about 84° points; a 0.42 mm line rounds them, which adds about 0.13 mm more
  (geometry with a 0.21 mm tip radius: 4.84-4.90 at no compensation, 5.34-5.40 at +0.20) **[I]**.
- **Risk:** at +0.20 per side the neck passes 5.3 and the pin could hop between lobes, a false fail of the pivot.
  Plastic cusps under squeeze load may also dent and let the pin move, which the metal part won't do **[I]**.
- **Fix (printing):** keep the frame's hole compensation at or under +0.15 per side. If the pin holes still print
  tight at that value, open them with a 1/4" drill by hand (Fabrication's own instruction **[F]**) rather than raise
  compensation. Add one lobed hole to coupon C4 (proposal below) and check the neck with the real pin: it must not
  pass between lobes without being pulled out.

## 3. Grip sleeve bottom end cap thins to 0.52 mm

- **Evidence:** the end cap that wraps under the plate's bottom is 2.26 mm thick at the bed (front) side but thins to
  **0.52 mm** at print height 16.5-16.9 mm, and is under 0.85 mm (two lines) for 23 layers, z 14.1-18.5 mm, at the
  plate's rounded back-bottom corner **[M]**. Everywhere else the sleeve wall over the metal is 1.52 mm (`SLV_T`)
  **[M]**. The trigger sleeve's end cap is a uniform 1.53 mm **[M]**.
- **Cause:** `GS1` puts the sleeve end `SLV_T` below the plate bottom on the grip centreline. The end face is square to
  the raked grip axis (about 9°), the plate bottom is level, so toward the back the gap closes to 0.52 mm at the
  plate's R0.2 corner **[M]**/**[I]**.
- **Why it matters:** this is a real-use part, and the bottom of the grip is what hits the bench when the tool is set
  down **[I]**. Bambu Studio's default wall generator is Arachne (variable width); "Detect thin wall" (single line,
  "Maybe printed not very well, because it's not closed loop") is off by default **[S]** (Bambu Studio
  `PrintConfig.cpp`, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/master/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp).
  So expect one narrow line there, or a gap **[I]**.
- **Fix (Fabrication, inbox note):** move the sleeve end so the end cap is at least `SLV_T` everywhere (about 1 mm
  further down the axis, or set `GS1` from the plate outline's closest point to the end face), and add a
  `templates.py` check for the minimum sleeve wall. **For the v0.95 print (printing):** v0.95 stays locked, so print it
  as is; in the slicer preview check layers 71-93 at the bottom end for a hole, and record what the end looks like.

## 4. Sleeve slots are open notches

- **Evidence:** neither sleeve has a closed hole in any layer **[M]**: below the slot ceiling each layer is a U, open at
  the bed side and one end. Bambu's XY hole compensation only acts on closed holes; open notches only get contour
  compensation **[S]** `accuracy.md` (S1). Contour compensation shrinking the outline would widen these slots, the
  opposite of a plate **[I]** (tooltip: "Contour of object will be grown or shrunk", `PrintConfig.cpp` above **[S]**).
- **The slot mouth is the first layer:** both slots (3.429 mm, 3.43-3.50 band **[F]**) open onto the bed, so
  elephant foot flares into the mouth. Elephant foot compensation shrinks only the initial layer **[S]**
  (`PrintConfig.cpp`). A 0.15 mm flare each side would leave about 3.13 mm, under the 3.20 printed plate **[I]**.
- **Coupon gap:** C4's 3.43 slot is a closed slot in a flat plate, so it does not predict either of these **[I]**.
- **Fix (printing):** add C4b (below) and deburr the slot mouth with a knife before fitting. **Suggestion
  (Fabrication):** a 0.5 mm x 45° chamfer on the slot mouth of both sleeves; it also guides the sleeve on.

## 5. Sleeve screw holes are horizontal

- **Evidence:** the two #7 (5.105 mm) holes through each sleeve's side walls lie along the bed; they show as the only
  sub-45° overhangs, at z 10.8-11.4 (trigger) and 12.7-13.5 (grip) **[M]**. They are open in each layer, so hole
  compensation doesn't reach them **[S]** (as in 4). Horizontal holes come out small at the top "due to material
  hanging down" **[S]** (CNC Kitchen, `design-strength.md`). Their height also depends on how much the slot-ceiling
  bridge sags, which sets where the sleeve sits on the plate edge **[I]**.
- **Fix (printing):** after fitting each sleeve to its plate, run a #7 (0.201") or 13/64" drill through plate and
  sleeve together. Band 5.0-5.3 **[F]**. No design change.

## 6. One hole compensation value per object

- **Evidence:** in Bambu Studio's source, `xy_hole_compensation`, `xy_contour_compensation` and
  `elefant_foot_compensation` sit in `PrintObjectConfig`, not `PrintRegionConfig` **[S]**
  (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/master/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.hpp). Modifiers change
  region settings, so a modifier can't give one hole its own value **[I]**. This corrects `accuracy.md`, which
  suggested a per-part modifier.
- **Consequence:** the collar carries a 6.35 pin hole, two 5.105 holes, four 3.25 slots and the 34.9 x 52.1 mm
  keyhole **[M]** on one value. Users report big holes looser than small ones on one value **[S]** (`accuracy.md`
  S16, forum). The keyhole edges must be within 0.1 mm **[F]**.
- **Fix (printing):** measure the keyhole (seat 28.58, big hole 34.93, neck 28.58 drawn) on the printed collar and
  record it; if it is out of band, send the numbers to Fabrication instead of splitting the collar. Per-object values
  do work: give each plate its own value if the coupons call for it.

## 7. Grip sleeve pocket is drawn 0.076 mm off the plate outline

- **Evidence:** the grip sleeve's pocket is the frame outline grown by 0.003 in (0.076 mm) per side (`sleeves.py`).
  Outline tolerance on both printed parts is 0.2-0.3 mm **[F]**, and the pocket's back face is a near-flat ceiling at
  z 22.5-23.1 mm that prints as stepped bridges **[M]**.
- **Risk:** the sleeve stops short or tilts on the printed (or laser-cut) plate and sits proud by a few tenths, which
  widens the grip span a little (54/55/55/53 mm, target 51-55 **[M]** `templates.py`). It can always be put on: the
  pocket is open forward and up **[I]**.
- **Fix (printing):** record how far it sits proud; sand the pocket if needed. **Suggestion (Fabrication):** 0.2 mm
  per side for a printed pocket.

## 8. Warping of the flat plates

- **Evidence:** frame 108 x 164 mm, trigger 89 x 147 mm, collar 69 x 97 mm at only 2.2 mm thick **[M]**. PETG warps
  more than PLA; the fixes are glue and a brim, and more walls and infill raise warping risk **[S]** `petg.md`.
- **Fix (printing):** glue stick and the air deflector (plan T3). Brim only if T4/T5 corners lift: then "Painted"
  brim ears away from the tabs, slots and pin holes. For the sleeves use "Outer brim only", never an inner brim, which
  would fill the slot **[S]** (brim types in `PrintConfig.cpp`).

## 9. Shims

- **Evidence:** pivot shim bore 6.731 mm over a 6.35 pin, keeper-post shim bore 5.080 mm over a 4.76 post (3/16");
  both 0.80 mm thick, 4 layers **[M]**. Uncompensated holes print up to 0.35 mm small **[S]**, which would leave the
  keeper-post shim at about 4.73, under the post **[I]**.
- **Fix (printing):** print them on the fit plate with the fit profile's compensation, and print 2 spares of each
  (0.1 g each). First-layer flare is a quarter of a 4-layer part: check the bore entry.

## 10. Sleeves for real use: load, orientation, material

- **Job:** the hand squeezes the round face toward the plate edge; fingers slide along the length; the tool gets knocked
  and set down; paint runoff and sometimes thinner; room to warm-car temperatures **[I]** (carlie's answers in `STATE.md`).
- **Orientation (keep as exported):** squeeze is compression straight into the plate edge through the roof layers,
  which doesn't peel layers apart **[I]**. Bending along the sleeve's length runs along the layers. The weak case is a
  side knock prying a wall outward: that loads the layer bond at the slot-top height, where each trigger wall is about
  2.96 mm wide **[M]**, across layers, where PETG Matte keeps 31 % of its impact strength **[S]** (TDS,
  `design-strength.md`). Lying on its side would need supports under the round face and turn the slot into a
  horizontal hole, so the current way is right **[I]**.
- **Walls:** over the metal the roof is 1.52 mm at centre and 1.30 mm at the slot corners **[M]**, about 3 lines; the
  trigger roof grows to 3.55 mm at the bow. Side walls 6.2 mm. `process-sleeve-0.20-v1` (4 walls, 25 % infill) leaves
  the roof all wall and top shell **[I]**; check in the preview that no sparse infill shows over the slot.
- **Material:** PETG stays right until T13 (paint/thinner) says otherwise **[S]** `petg.md`. HDT 71 °C **[S]**: don't
  leave the tool on a hot dashboard **[I]**.
- **Comfort:** the hand faces are the top domes, so they show layer steps where the dome flattens. Variable layer
  height on the sleeves only above the slot ceiling would smooth them; optional, decide after the first print **[I]**.

## 11. Cross-checks

- `check3d.py`: closest free pairs are 0.047" (1.19 mm, can | collar) and 0.060" (1.52 mm, handle plate | trigger)
  **[M]**. The 0.2-0.3 mm print tolerance on outlines **[F]** doesn't use those up.
- `MEASUREMENTS.md`: the real can's lip gap is 2.58 mm; the collar slices to 2.20, leaving 0.38 mm **[M]**.
- The trigger stack: printed plates at 3.20 instead of 3.175 use 0.025 mm of the 1.65 mm side play **[M]**.
